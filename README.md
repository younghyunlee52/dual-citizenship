
# What Can Dual Citizens Teach Us About Political Engagement?

**Seyoung Jung, Younghyun Lee, and Cara Wong**  
*Political Science Research and Methods* (2025)

[Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1017/psrm.2025.10053) |
[Replication materials](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/9XBRQS)

## Overview

How do citizenship status and political socialization shape political engagement? We compare U.S.-born dual citizens with naturalized dual citizens and U.S.-born mono citizens to distinguish these dimensions.

Our findings show that political engagement varies with both nativity and citizenship status, and that patterns differ between voting and other political activities.

## Data and Methods

- **Data:** 2020 and 2022 Cooperative Election Study (CES).
- **Design:** Two paired comparisons of citizenship groups.
- **Matching:** Mahalanobis-distance matching with calipers, supplemented by exact matching to improve comparability.
- **Robustness checks:** Additional political covariates, validated
  turnout, and geographic and residential characteristics.
